## 💡 Pro Tips for Success

1. **Write longer blog posts** (2,500+ words)
   - More time on page = better signals
   - More opportunity for internal links
   - More valuable to readers

2. **Internal linking is crucial**
   - Every blog post: link to 10-15 prompts
   - Every prompt: link to 3-5 related prompts
   - Keeps users on site longer

3. **Update old content regularly**
   - Shows Google the site is actively maintained
   - Change "published date" to recent date
   - Add new examples/screenshots

4. **Focus on organic SEO from day one**
   - Write for keywords (not just volume)
   - Use long-tail keywords (less competition)
   - Optimize titles and meta descriptions

5. **Build an email list from the start**
   - Offer free prompt template in signup
   - Builds recurring audience
   - Shows community interest

6. **Publish consistently**
   - Not sporadic
   - 1-2 posts per week is perfect
   - Builds Google trust in your site
