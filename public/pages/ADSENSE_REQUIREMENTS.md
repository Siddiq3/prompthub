# Google AdSense Approval Requirements & Rejection Reasons

## 🚨 Why Most Sites Get REJECTED (Top Reasons)

### Reason #1: Insufficient Original Content (40% of rejections)
**What Google sees**: Your site is mostly just a database of short snippets or copied content

**Red flags**:
- Fewer than 10 pages with meaningful content
- Average page length < 300 words
- Thin descriptions or auto-generated content
- Heavy reliance on user-generated content without curation
- Content that's copied from other prompt sites

**How you fix it**:
- ✅ Write 300+ word original descriptions for EVERY prompt
- ✅ Create 20+ blog posts demonstrating expertise
- ✅ Show unique perspectives (not rehashed content)
- ✅ Include your own examples and experiences
- ✅ Update content regularly (shows active maintenance)

---

### Reason #2: Content Policy Violations (25% of rejections)
**What Google sees**: Potentially risky content that advertisers avoid

**Red flags**:
- Prompts for creating fake/harmful imagery
- Adult or sexually suggestive content in descriptions
- Excessive swearing or inappropriate language
- Content that could be used for fraud/forgery
- Prompts encouraging dangerous activities
- Copyright infringement in your descriptions

**How you fix it**:
- ✅ Review all prompts for adult content
- ✅ Remove prompts for fake IDs, deepfakes, misinformation
- ✅ Keep language professional and family-friendly
- ✅ Include disclaimer: "Generated images are AI creations, not real photos"
- ✅ Don't claim AI images are real or authentic
- ✅ Add copyright notice about responsible use

---

### Reason #3: Inadequate Pages (20% of rejections)
**Missing pages cause automatic rejection**

**REQUIRED pages (must have ALL FOUR)**:
1. ✅ **About page** - Who you are, why this site exists (500+ words recommended)
2. ✅ **Privacy Policy** - How you collect and use data (comprehensive, specific)
3. ✅ **Terms of Service** - Rules for using your site
4. ✅ **Contact page** - Way to reach you (email, contact form, or both)

**Why these matter**:
- About page = transparency (shows you're legitimate)
- Privacy Policy = GDPR/CCPA compliance (legal requirement)
- Terms = defines acceptable use (protects you)
- Contact = accountability (shows you're reachable)

**How to do it right**:
- Include detailed about-us information (not generic)
- Privacy policy must be comprehensive, not a template
- Terms should be business-specific (about AI prompts)
- Contact should be easy to use (tested it yourself?)

---

### Reason #4: Poor User Experience (15% of rejections)

**Red flags**:
- Site not mobile-friendly
- Slow loading times (>3 seconds)
- Broken links
- Excessive ads/popups (on initial review)
- Poor navigation
- Difficult to find information

**How you fix it**:
- ✅ Mobile test: View on iPhone, Android, tablet
- ✅ Speed test: Google PageSpeed Insights (target: 75+)
- ✅ Check all links work (run link checker tool)
- ✅ Clear navigation menu
- ✅ Remove aggressive popups BEFORE approval
- ✅ Organize content logically

---

### Reason #5: Low Traffic/Authority (10% of rejections)

**Red flags**:
- Brand new site with zero traffic
- Site that's existed for <6 months
- Very few backlinks
- Not indexed in Google
- Minimal social media presence

**How to fix it** (before applying):
- ✅ Get indexed in Google (submit sitemap)
- ✅ Wait at least 6 months after launching
- ✅ Build organic traffic to 1,000+ monthly visitors
- ✅ Create content worth linking to
- ✅ Social media presence (at least active on one platform)
- ✅ Get some backlinks (expert interviews, guest posts)

## What Google's Human Reviewers Actually Check

When your application goes to a real person at Google, they specifically look for:

### The 5-Minute Test
1. **Does the site feel legit?** (professional design, not spammy)
2. **Can I find the contact info easily?** (About, Contact, Privacy visible)
3. **Is there meaningful content?** (not just trying to sell ads)
4. **Does it look like a real business?** (branding, professionalism)
5. **Is it safe for advertisers?** (no risky content)

### Content Deep Dive
- Read your About page (assesses legitimacy)
- Skim 3-5 blog posts (checks for quality)
- Spot check a few prompts (original content?)
- Look for plagiarism (might run Copyscape)
- Check if site is indexed in Google
- Look at your traffic metrics (is it real?)

### Technical Checklist
- Site loads quickly
- Mobile version looks good
- No broken links
- SSL/HTTPS present
- Privacy policy is comprehensive
- Terms of Service present

## Common Rejection Messages & Solutions

### "Insufficient Content"
**Problem**: Not enough original writing
**Solution**: 
- Add 300+ word descriptions to all prompts
- Write 20 blog posts (each 2,000+ words)
- Ensure everything is original (not copied)
- Reapply in 30 days

### "Ineligible Content"
**Problem**: Some content violates policies
**Solution**:
- Identify risky prompts (deepfakes, fake IDs, etc.)
- Remove them completely (don't just hide)
- Review all content for policy violations
- Reapply in 30 days

### "Incomplete Privacy Policy"
**Problem**: Missing or too brief
**Solution**:
- Write comprehensive privacy policy (2,000+ words)
- Specifically mention Google AdSense cookies
- Include GDPR/CCPA sections
- Make it specific to your business (not generic)

### "Site Not Approved for Ads"
**Problem**: Domain or hosting issues
**Solution**:
- Check domain age (must be 6+ months)
- Verify HTTPS/SSL working
- Check robots.txt isn't blocking crawlers
- Verify site is indexed in Google Search Console

### "Traffic Issue" (implied rejection)
**Problem**: Site gets almost no traffic
**Solution**:
- Wait and build organic traffic first
- Publish high-quality blog content
- Get indexed in Google
- Build to at least 1,000 monthly visitors
- Then reapply

### "Reapplication Rejected Too"
**Problem**: You fixed one issue, Google found another
**Common second-wave issues**:
- Ads will cover too much content (too many ads planned)
- Copied content (even small sections)
- Still have policy-violating content
- Site still too new/low traffic

**Solution**: Contact AdSense support, ask specifically what issue remains

## After Approval: Important Rules

Once you're approved, DON'T:
- ❌ Place ads before verification (wait for approval email)
- ❌ Violate policies with new content
- ❌ Click your own ads (it's fraud)
- ❌ Ask others to click your ads (it's fraud)
- ❌ Use misleading ad placement ("click here for prize")
- ❌ Have excessive ads (Google will suspend)
- ❌ Bot traffic or fake clicks
- ❌ Hide ads in confusing layouts

DO:
- ✅ Optimize ad placements for best revenue
- ✅ Continue creating quality content
- ✅ Monitor for policy violations
- ✅ Track earnings and analytics
- ✅ Update content regularly
- ✅ Respond to user comments

**Violation = Account suspension = No more earnings (permanently sometimes)**

Make sure your site stays compliant!
