# 30-Day AdSense Approval Action Plan

**Current situation**: You have a working prompt library website

### Days 5-7: Batch Update Existing Prompts (5-6 hours)

**Task**: Take your current best-performing prompts and expand descriptions to 300+ words

**How to do it efficiently**:

1. **Pick your top 30 prompts** (by views, if you have analytics)

2. **Use the template** from PROMPT_TEMPLATE.md document
   - Introduction (50-75 words)
   - The prompt (full text)
   - How to use (80-120 words)
   - When to use (60-80 words)
   - Customization tips (70-100 words)
   - Platform compatibility
   - Quick FAQ (50-100 words)
   - **Total: 300-500 words**

3. **Speed-up technique**: Batch similar prompts
   - Group by category (sports, landscapes, portraits, etc.)
   - Write base descriptions, then customize for each
   - This is faster than individual writing

4. **Every description must**:
   - ✅ Be original (you write it)
   - ✅ Show expertise (specific, detailed)
   - ✅ Include how-to guidance
   - ✅ Have internal links to related prompts
   - ✅ Mention platform differences
   - ✅ Be proofread

5. **Update schedule**:
   - Day 5: Write 10 descriptions
   - Day 6: Write 10 descriptions
   - Day 7: Write 10 descriptions + proofread all 30

**Pro tip**: Use a template system:
- Copy-paste the structure
- Fill in [PROMPT_NAME], [PLATFORM], etc.
- Write the unique parts
- Takes 15-20 min per prompt once you have a rhythm

**Deliverables**:
- 30 expanded prompt descriptions (300+ words each)
- All descriptions have internal links
- Proofread and published

**Time**: 5-6 hours

## After Day 30: The Waiting Game

### You're Now Ready to Apply for AdSense!

**But wait** before you hit apply:

1. **Verify traffic metrics**:
   - Check Google Analytics: Do you have 1,000+ monthly visitors?
   - If no: Wait another 30 days, publish 10 more blog posts
   - If yes: Proceed to apply

2. **Final checklist**:
   - All 4 required pages present? ✅
   - 50+ indexed pages? ✅
   - Original content throughout? ✅
   - No policy violations? ✅
   - Mobile-friendly? ✅
   - Fast loading? ✅

3. **Submit AdSense Application**:
   - Go to https://adsense.google.com
   - Click "Sign Up"
   - Enter your website URL
   - Follow the steps
   - Verify your site (add code snippet)

### Timeline After Application

- **Days 1-7**: Automated system review
- **Days 8-21**: Human review (real person looks at your site)
- **Days 22-30**: Decision made (maybe approval, maybe rejection with reason)

**Typical approval timeline**: 30-90 days (be patient!)

---

## If You Get Rejected

**Don't panic.** 80% of rejections are fixable.

**Most common reasons**:
1. Insufficient content → Add 5-10 more blog posts
2. Thin descriptions → Expand all prompts to 500+ words
3. Policy violation → Remove problematic content
4. Low traffic → Build to 1,000+ visitors first

**Next steps if rejected**:
1. Read rejection reason carefully
2. Fix the issue(s)
3. Wait 30 days
4. Reapply

**You can reapply multiple times.** Don't give up after rejection.
