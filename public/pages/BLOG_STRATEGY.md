# Blog Content Strategy for AdSense & Organic Traffic

## Why You MUST Have a Blog (AdSense Requirement)

Google AdSense reviewers specifically look for:
1. **Original written content** demonstrating expertise
2. **Regular publishing schedule** showing active maintenance
3. **Valuable information** users can't find anywhere else
4. **Organic traffic potential** (tracked via Google Search Console)
5. **Engagement signals** (time on page, shares, comments)

A thin prompt database without blog content is **rejected for AdSense 90% of the time**.

## SEO Metrics to Track

**In Google Search Console**:
- Track impressions and CTR for each post
- Monitor ranking positions for target keywords
- Check for indexing errors
- Analyze search queries people use to find you

**Analytics Goals**:
- Average time on page: 3+ minutes (shows value)
- Bounce rate: <50% (shows relevance)
- Pages per session: 3+ (shows internal linking working)
- Return visitor rate: 25%+ (shows loyal audience)

**Signs Your AdSense Application Will Be Approved**:
- ✅ 50+ indexed pages of original content
- ✅ 5,000+ monthly organic visitors
- ✅ Average session duration 2+ minutes
- ✅ All 4 required pages present
- ✅ Clear, honest policies
- ✅ Regular content updates
- ✅ No plagiarized or thin content
- ✅ Good mobile experience
- ✅ Fast page load times
- ✅ Proper schema markup
