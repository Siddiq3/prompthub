# SEO-Optimized Prompt Description Template & Guide

## Why 300+ Word Descriptions Matter for AdSense

Google AdSense reviewers look for **original, valuable content** on every page. Thin descriptions (under 100 words) are red flags for:
- Low-quality, auto-generated content sites
- Lack of genuine human expertise
- Content farms designed purely for ads (AdSense rejection reason #1)

Each prompt page should feel like an **educational resource**, not just a prompt lookup table.

Use this structure for every prompt you create:

(50-75 words)

Perfect for sports photographers, design agencies creating sports content, and marketing teams needing high-impact imagery. #### **The Prompt Itself**

#### **How to Use This Prompt**
(80-120 words)

Step-by-step guidance:
1. Copy the full prompt
2. Paste into [AI Platform]
3. Key parameters to adjust:
   - [Parameter 1]: When to use, how it affects output
   - [Parameter 2]: Best practices
   - [Parameter 3]: Common adjustments

Include specific settings, seed recommendations, or platform-specific tips.

*Example:*
> "Paste the entire prompt into Midjourney. Key adjustments:
> - **Aspect ratio**: Use --ar 16:9 for widescreen shots, --ar 3:2 for standard
> - **Quality**: Add --q 2 for maximum detail (uses 4x credits)
> - **Style strength**: Adjust 'cinematic lighting' intensity in parentheses for softer/harder shadows
> First-time results are usually excellent. If needed, regenerate 2-3 times to capture perfect action moment."

#### **When to Use This Prompt**
(60-80 words)

Specific real-world scenarios, project types, and industries where this prompt excels:
- Marketing and social media campaigns
- Blog and website hero images
- Print media and posters
- Stock photography alternatives
- Concept development and storyboarding

Include "avoid" scenarios where this prompt underperforms.

#### **Customization Tips**
(70-100 words)

How users can modify the prompt for different outcomes:
- What elements you can swap
- Style modifiers (lighting, composition, mood)
- Subject matter variations
- Platform-specific adjustments
- Parameter tweaking for different AI models

Include examples of successful variations.

*Example:*
> "Try these variations:
> - Replace 'cricket' with 'baseball' or 'tennis' for different sports
> - Swap 'daylight stadium' with 'night match under floodlights' for different lighting
> - Change 'action shot' to 'trophy celebration' for different moment
> - For Stable Diffusion, reduce technical terms and add 'high resolution, intricate details'
> - On DALL-E, focus on visual descriptors rather than technical parameters"

#### **Common Results & What to Expect**
(60-90 words)

Honest assessment of typical outputs:
- What this prompt consistently produces well
- Occasional variations or limitations
- Quality consistency across regenerations
- Best results from specific AI models
- Performance across different aspect ratios
- Typical processing time and quality tiers

Composition and framing are usually excellent. Occasional issues: player count (may add/remove extras), equipment details (bat/ball positioning). Best on Midjourney with --q 2. Average process time: 60 seconds. DALL-E produces more stylized, slightly less realistic results."

## Content Writing Best Practices for AdSense Approval

### ✅ DO:
- **Write 300-500 words minimum** per prompt
- **Use your own voice** and expertise
- **Include real examples** of what to expect
- **Provide genuine value** beyond just the prompt
- **Be transparent** about limitations and results variation
- **Include platform-specific guidance**
- **Update based on AI model changes**
- **Link to related content** (internal linking is crucial)

### ❌ DON'T:
- **Spin content** from other prompt sites
- **Use thin, auto-generated descriptions**
- **Copy prompts wholesale** from competitors
- **Overpromise results** ("This ALWAYS creates perfect images")
- **Ignore technical details**
- **Repeat the same content** across similar prompts
- **Use low-quality placeholder descriptions**
- **Fill space with fluff**

## Real Example: Completed Prompt Description

### Cricket Trophy Celebration Photo — Midjourney & Flux Prompt

**What This Prompt Does**

This prompt generates stunning photorealistic images of cricket trophy celebrations, featuring athletes holding trophies amid stadium confetti, champagne, and dramatic lighting. It's ideal for sports photographers, design agencies creating championship graphics, Instagram content creators, and marketing teams needing celebratory sports imagery. The prompt produces championship-quality results with excellent composition, professional lighting, and authentic celebration details.

**The Prompt**

```
Professional cricket match trophy celebration, triumphant athlete holding golden trophy above head, confetti explosion, champagne bottle, stadium background with cheering crowd, dramatic evening lighting, cinematic composition, dynamic depth of field, photorealistic, award ceremony, championship moment, vibrant colors, emotional peak action --ar 16:9 --q 2
```

**How to Use This Prompt**

1. Copy the complete prompt above
2. Open Midjourney and paste into Discord command
3. Press Enter and wait 60-90 seconds
4. Key adjustments:
   - **--ar 16:9**: Creates widescreen shots perfect for social media
   - **--q 2**: Doubles quality details (uses 4x image credits, but worth it)
   - **--niji 6**: For more stylized, anime-influenced versions
5. If results don't capture the celebration moment perfectly, regenerate (use the 🔄 button) 2-3 times

**When to Use This Prompt**

- **Sports marketing**: Victory announcements, championship campaigns
- **Social media**: Instagram stories, LinkedIn sport content
- **Website design**: Hero images for sports brands, event pages
- **Print media**: Magazine covers, promotional posters
- **Concept art**: Storyboarding celebratory scenes
- **Blog illustrations**: Sports achievement articles

Avoid using when you need indoor formal ceremonies (this prompt excels with stadium/crowd elements).
